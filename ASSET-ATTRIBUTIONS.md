# Asset attributions

All photographs are locally bundled, optimized WebP files. They are stock architectural inspiration, not photographs of actual Dubai listings. Property names and listings in this demonstration are fictional. The photograph subjects and original locations are described below to avoid implying these are the advertised properties.

## Fonts

Cormorant Garamond and Manrope are locally hosted Latin variable fonts obtained from Google Fonts. Their respective SIL Open Font License texts are included beside the fonts in `public/fonts/`. Font binaries are distributed unmodified.
